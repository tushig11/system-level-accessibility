# System-Level Accessibility  
## Growing Teams (16–50 People)

---

## Focus

Ensure consistency across teams and systems.

---

## Key Layers

- Distribution  
- Preservation  
- Enforcement  
- Boundary  
- Continuity  

---

## Practices

- central design system  
- migration accessibility strategy  
- CI/CD enforcement  
- third-party governance  
- knowledge sharing  

---

- inconsistent implementations

- unmanaged third-party tools  

---

## Outcome

Accessibility becomes a scalable organizational capability.
